How dress.me works

One search. Hundreds of retailers.

Start searching
01

Describe it in plain English

Type exactly what you're looking for — "oversized navy wool coat under £150" or "floral midi dress for a wedding". No need to browse category trees or know the right keywords.

02

AI searches hundreds of retailers

Claude AI rewrites your query into multiple search variations and pulls results from hundreds of UK and international retailers — all in a few seconds. Results are deduplicated and ranked for you.

03

Filter to find your match

Refine by colour, gender, category, size, and price. Use the keyword filter to narrow further — "linen only" or "under £80". Your filters apply instantly.

04

Save what you love

Save items as you browse. Everything is stored in your browser — no account required, nothing sent to our servers. Share your saved list with anyone via a single link.

05

Build outfit boards

Group saved items into named outfit boards. Share a board with a single link. Or use the outfit generator to build a complete look from a single description — no account required.

Common questions

Is dress.me free?

Yes. dress.me is free to use. We earn a small commission when you click through to a retailer and make a purchase — at no extra cost to you.

Do I need an account?

No account required. Your saved items, outfits, and preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are never sent to our servers.

Which retailers does dress.me search?

dress.me searches Google Shopping, which covers thousands of UK and international retailers including ASOS, Marks & Spencer, Zara, H&M, Farfetch, Next, John Lewis, and many more.

Are prices accurate?

Prices shown are fetched live from Google Shopping and are generally accurate, but always check the retailer's website for the current price before purchasing.
