How dress.me works

One search. Hundreds of retailers. Powered by AI.

Start searching
🔍

Describe it in plain English

Type exactly what you're looking for — "oversized navy wool coat under £150" or "floral midi dress for a wedding". Our AI understands natural language and expands your search to find the best matches across the web.

✨

AI expands your search

Behind the scenes, Claude AI rewrites your query into multiple search variations, then pulls results from hundreds of UK and international retailers — all in a few seconds. You see the best results, deduplicated and ranked.

🎯

Filter to find your match

Refine by colour, gender, category, size, and price. Use the keyword filter to narrow further — "linen only" or "with pockets". Your filters are remembered as you search.

♡

Save and organise

Save items as you browse. Organise them into named collections. Add notes ("try in store first") and share your saved list with a link — no account required. Everything is stored in your browser.

👗

Build outfit boards

Group saved items into outfit boards. Ask the AI to suggest what else might complete the look. Share your outfit boards with a single link.

📷

Virtually try it on

Upload a photo of yourself and try on any item from the results. Powered by Fashn.ai. Your photo is never stored — it is only used for the try-on and discarded immediately after.

Common questions

Is dress.me free?

Yes. dress.me is free to use. We earn a small commission when you click through to a retailer and make a purchase — at no extra cost to you.

Do I need an account?

No account required. Your saved items, outfits, and preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are never sent to our servers.

Which retailers does dress.me search?

dress.me searches Google Shopping, which covers thousands of UK and international retailers including ASOS, Marks & Spencer, Zara, H&M, Farfetch, Next, John Lewis, and many more.

Are prices accurate?

Prices shown are fetched live from Google Shopping and are generally accurate, but always check the retailer's website for the current price before purchasing.

How does the virtual try-on work?

You upload a photo of yourself and select an item. We send both to Fashn.ai, which composites the garment onto your image using AI. Your photo is used only for this purpose and is not stored.
