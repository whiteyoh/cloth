Privacy Notice

Last updated: 14 August 2026

What we collect

dress.me does not create user accounts. We do not store your search queries in a persistent database. Searches are held in an in-memory cache for up to 30 minutes to improve speed and reduce external API calls; the cache is lost when the server restarts.

We log a one-way hash (SHA-256) of your search query for diagnostic and reliability purposes. This hash cannot be reversed to recover your original query.

Third-party processors

When you search, your query is sent to SerpAPI (serpapi.com) to retrieve shopping results from Google Shopping. SerpAPI processes your query on servers outside the UK. We are in the process of verifying SerpAPI's data processing agreement for UK GDPR compliance (see our risk register).

dress.me is hosted on Render.com. Platform-level logs may include your IP address as part of standard server operation. We are verifying Render.com's data processing agreement for UK GDPR compliance.

Cookies

dress.me does not currently use cookies or tracking scripts. If this changes, this notice will be updated before any cookies are set.

Virtual try-on

dress.me offers an optional virtual try-on feature that lets you see how a clothing item looks on you. Using this feature is entirely voluntary.

  • What data is collected: when you use virtual try-on, your uploaded photo and the garment image URL are transmitted to the Fashn.ai API. No other personal data is collected as part of this request.
  • Third-party processor: your photo is processed by Fashn.ai (fashn.ai), a commercial AI service. dress.me uses Fashn.ai solely to generate the composited try-on image. We recommend reviewing Fashn.ai's own privacy policy for details of how they handle uploaded data.
  • Retention: dress.me does not store your photo beyond the duration of the try-on request. Once the result image URL is returned to your browser, the upload is discarded. The result image is hosted by Fashn.ai; dress.me does not cache or retain it.
  • Legal basis: processing is based on your explicit consent, given via the consent checkbox displayed at the point of upload ("Your photo is processed to generate the result and is not stored or shared"). You must tick this box before each try-on request.
  • Your rights: as this feature processes a photo of you, it may involve biometric-category data under UK GDPR. You can withdraw consent at any time simply by not using the try-on feature. For questions about data processed by Fashn.ai, contact them directly. For any other queries, contact us at privacy@cloth.example.
  • Feature availability: virtual try-on is only available when the feature is enabled by the site operator. When not enabled, no upload controls are presented and no data is transmitted.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, and erase personal data we hold about you. Because we do not link queries to identifiable individuals, we hold no personal data about you beyond platform logs (which are outside our direct control). For queries about platform log data, contact us at privacy@cloth.example.
