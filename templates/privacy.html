Privacy Notice

Last updated: 14 August 2026

What we collect

dress.me does not create user accounts. We do not store your search queries in a persistent database. Searches are held in an in-memory cache for up to 30 minutes to improve speed and reduce external API calls; the cache is lost when the server restarts.

We log a one-way hash (SHA-256) of your search query for diagnostic and reliability purposes. This hash cannot be reversed to recover your original query.

Third-party processors

When you search, your query is sent to SerpAPI (serpapi.com) to retrieve shopping results from Google Shopping. SerpAPI processes your query on servers outside the UK. We are in the process of verifying SerpAPI's data processing agreement for UK GDPR compliance (see our risk register).

dress.me is hosted on Render.com. Platform-level logs may include your IP address as part of standard server operation. We are verifying Render.com's data processing agreement for UK GDPR compliance.

Cookies

dress.me itself does not set cookies. However, Skimlinks (our affiliate partner) may set cookies for commission tracking purposes. See the Affiliate links section below for details and opt-out options.

Affiliate links

dress.me uses Skimlinks (publisher ID 307776X1796068) to manage affiliate relationships. When you click a product link on dress.me, Skimlinks may rewrite the link to include an affiliate tracking parameter. If you then make a purchase, dress.me may receive a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Skimlinks may set cookies or use similar technologies to track clicks for commission purposes. You can find more detail in Skimlinks' privacy policy. You can opt out of Skimlinks tracking via their opt-out page.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, and erase personal data we hold about you. Because we do not link queries to identifiable individuals, we hold no personal data about you beyond platform logs (which are outside our direct control). For queries about platform log data, contact us at hello@dress.me.
